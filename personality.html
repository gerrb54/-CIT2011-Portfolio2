My Strongest Traits and Hobbies

Gaming is one of my favorite hobbies because it allows me to have fun, relax, and challenge myself. I enjoy playing different types of games that require me to think, make decisions, and solve problems. Gaming also connects with my interest in computers and technology. In my school life, it has helped me develop problem-solving skills and has increased my interest in learning more about how computers and programs work.

Socializing

It also helps me with my communication skills and makes it easier for me to work with other students during group assignments and projects.

Why Spider-Man Is My Hero

Spider-Man is my hero because he shows the importance of responsibility, determination, and helping others. Even when situations become difficult, he continues trying to do what he believes is right. I admire this because it reminds me that having skills or abilities is not enough; what matters is how you choose to use them. This motivates me to keep working toward my goals and to use what I learn to help others.

Using AI as a Tool, Not a Crutch

To me, using AI as a tool and not a crutch means using it to help you understand your work instead of allowing it to do everything for you. Not everyone will understand everything that a teacher explains the first time. AI can help by simplifying the information and explaining it in a way that may be easier to understand. This allows students to actually learn what they are doing instead of simply getting the answers.

AI has helped me understand coding and other difficult topics better. I have used it to help me find mistakes in code that I wrote and to explain topics that I found difficult. For example, when I was doing Programming II, I sometimes found topics such as functions difficult to understand. Having a difficult topic explained in simpler terms can make it easier for me to understand what I am learning.

However, depending on AI too much can cause someone to lose or not properly develop their own skills. For example, if someone constantly depends on AI while using Microsoft Word, they may not learn how to use the program properly by themselves. The same thing can happen to a developer who depends on AI to write code all the time. If the developer is not practicing and understanding the code, their own coding skills may become weaker.

I believe students should use AI to assist them in areas that they do not understand based on what was explained by their teacher. Students should still attempt their work, practice their skills, and make sure they understand what they are doing. AI should support the learning process rather than replace the student's own thinking and effort.
