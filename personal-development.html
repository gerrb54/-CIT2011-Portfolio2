Personal Development

My reflection on lessons from The Richest Man in Babylon.

Seven Cures Five Laws of Gold My Biggest Takeaway

The Seven Cures for a Lean Purse

The Seven Cures for a Lean Purse made me think more about the way I save and spend my money. I would say that I am okay at saving, but sometimes when I really want something, I will go into my savings even if I do not necessarily need what I am buying. One thing I would like to change is how often I do this and to stop spending money on things that are not necessary.

Controlling my spending is also important to me because I sometimes spend money on food when I could eat or cook at home instead. Cutting down on spending like this would allow me to keep more of the money that I save.

Another lesson that stood out to me was increasing my ability to earn. Since I am studying Computer Science, I believe that improving my skills and getting experience can create more opportunities for me in the future. I would like to gain experience through summer jobs, whether in Jamaica or overseas. This could help me develop my Computer Science skills while also improving my ability to earn money.

The Five Laws of Gold

From the Five Laws of Gold, I learned that it is important to be careful with the money that I have. Right now, I would rather focus on saving my money because if I invest too much of it, I may be left with very little money for myself. Saving allows me to gradually build up the amount of money that I have.

I also would not give someone $20,000 just because they promised that they could double it quickly. Sometimes a person may take months to return the money even though they said it would happen quickly, or the person could simply scam you. I think it is better not to take unnecessary risks with money because that $20,000 could have been used for something important. If I ever decide to invest, I would want to understand what I am investing in first and avoid risking all of my savings.

My Biggest Takeaway

My biggest takeaway is that I need to save more consistently and avoid using money unless it is necessary. In the future, I will have responsibilities such as paying bills, buying groceries, taking care of my family, paying for a home, and possibly owning a car. Having money saved would help me deal with these responsibilities.

I also realized that there are things I spend money on that I could reduce, such as junk food, buying food when I could eat at home, and subscriptions that I barely use. These are expenses that can waste money over time. The main lesson I took from the book is that I should save whenever I can and become more disciplined about what I choose to spend my money on.
